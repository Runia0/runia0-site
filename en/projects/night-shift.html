3D game combining gas station and market management with psychological horror.

Play demo — NIGHT SHIFT: Son Vardiya

Demo: the first day and night (about 20 minutes).

Opens in the browser; keyboard and mouse recommended on desktop.

Overview

The player runs a gas station and market on the night shift; their choices affect both the business and an increasingly strange story.

The game is being built on web 3D (Three.js) following a six-stage plan. The current build covers the business, staff, story and horror stages; final polish is ongoing.

Highlights

  • Business simulation with fuel and stock orders, a cash register and customers
  • Staff management and security cameras
  • Branching dialogue and multiple endings
  • In-game phone, save system and settings

Next steps

  • Polish stage: visual detail, balancing and mobile controls
